## Browser-measured motion

Computed styles were measured on actual component elements. Reduced motion was emulated through the bound browser's supported CDP capability, then reset to no preference.

| Component | Normal | Reduced motion |
| --- | --- | --- |

| Spinner | `spin`, 1s linear; rotating matrix observed | `spin`, 2s linear; useful loading feedback remains visible at half the rotation speed |
| BouncingLoader | `bouncing-typing-wave`, 1.4s, delays 0/0.2/0.4s; sampled translations about -5.9px/-3.1px/0px | All three dots: no animation and no transform |

No additional spring or entrance motion was added to placeholders or loading content. These are continuous status indicators, so the restrained existing loops are appropriate. Completed content and labels remain still.
